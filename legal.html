Data & Security

Privacy Policy

V6 is designed to be privacy-first. We collect the minimum data necessary to run a global social network.

1 Information We Store

A. Social Identity & Profiles

We store the following in our secure Firebase infrastructure:

  • Email & Unique UID
  • Display Name & Username
  • Profile Picture (PFP) Data/URLs
  • School & District Affiliation
  • Follower/Following Relationships
  • Messenger Profiles & Public IDs

B. Real-Time Interactions

Messages, Dailyphoto posts (including images/GIFs), comments, and likes are stored in Firestore. This content is associated with your account but can be deleted at any time by the user.

C. Presence & Analytics

We track active sessions, page duration, and "Current Activity" (the page you are on) to provide real-time status updates to your mutual friends and maintain the global leaderboard.

2 Local-Only Data

The following data is stored strictly on your device via LocalStorage or IndexedDB and is never sent to our servers:

  • Notes: All written notes.
  • Schedules: Your class and bell schedules.
  • Customization: Local theme overrides.
  • Privacy: Panic Keys & Tab Disguise config.

3 Account Deletion

You have the right to disappear. Deleting your account via the Settings page will permanently wipe your Firestore document, authentication credentials, and associated social data from our primary database.
