4SP operates as an independent, decentralized network infrastructure primarily serving student communities. Your access to this network is a privilege granted by the platform administration and is contingent upon your adherence to these rules.

2. User Responsibility & Conduct

You are solely responsible for your conduct and any data, text, photos, audio, or other materials ("Content") that you transmit, submit, or display on the 4SP service.

  • No Malicious Activity: You may not distribute viruses, destructive code, or attempt to compromise the integrity of the Firebase infrastructure, Cloud Functions, or Bare/Wisp servers.
  • Identity Integrity: You may not spoof administrative accounts or falsely claim affiliation with the 4SP development team.
  • Respectful Interaction: Harassment, hate speech, threats, and explicit content are strictly prohibited across all social features.

3. Content Responsibility (Dailyphoto & Posts)

Features like Dailyphoto and user feeds operate on a hyper-local, community-driven basis (e.g., filtered by school or district).

4simpleproblems is NOT liable for user-uploaded imagery or text. We provide the conduit, but you retain ownership and responsibility for what you post. We reserve the right to remove any content reported by the community or deemed inappropriate by administrators without prior notice.

4. Global Leaderboard Terms

The 4SP Global Leaderboard is an opt-in, competitive ranking system that displays user activity and engagement metrics across the network.

  • Opt-In Consent: By explicitly opting into the leaderboard, you consent to the public display of your Display Name, Username, Profile Picture (PFP, Custom, or Mibi Avatar), School Affiliation, and your calculated Activity Score.
  • Score Calculation: Scores are generated via proprietary algorithms evaluating session duration, feature engagement, and social interactions. Exploiting bugs or using automated scripts (bots) to artificially inflate your score is prohibited and will result in a permanent ban.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may revoke your consent at any time via the Settings panel. Opting out will immediately remove your profile from the public leaderboard rankings.

5. Messenger & Social Graph

Our social features (Messenger, Follower/Following mechanics, Mutuals) facilitate peer-to-peer communication.

  • End-to-End Encryption: Currently, messages in 4SP Messenger are stored securely in Firestore but are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not share highly sensitive personal information.
  • User Controls: 4SP does not proactively monitor private messages. You are empowered and expected to use the Block, Unfriend, and Report tools to manage your own social boundaries.

7. Administration & Governance

The primary Creator (4simpleproblems@gmail.com) and appointed Administrators maintain absolute authority over the platform infrastructure.

Administrators possess the tools to globally ban users, instantly disintegrate accounts, and manage network traffic. Privacy Policy

Committed to minimal data retention.

1. Information We Store (Cloud Data)

To facilitate social networking, cross-device syncing, and authentication, we securely store the following data within our Google Firebase infrastructure:

Identity & Profile

  • Email Address & Authentication Provider (Google, MS, GitHub)
  • Display Name & @Username
  • Profile Visuals (URL, Custom Base64, or Mibi Config)
  • School/District Affiliation

Social & Interaction

  • Followers / Following Maps
  • Sent & Received Messages
  • Posts, Comments, and Dailyphotos
  • Notifications and Friend Requests

2. Local-Only Data (On-Device)

V6 respects your privacy by processing highly personal utility data entirely locally. The following information is stored in your browser's LocalStorage or IndexedDB and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Notes All data generated in the Notes application.
  • Schedules Your personalized class and bell schedules.
  • Privacy Settings Panic Key bindings and Tab Disguise configurations.
  • Local Themes Un-synced theme preferences and navbar layouts.

3. Analytics & Cookies

We utilize Google Analytics (GA4) to monitor broad traffic trends, feature usage, and network health. This helps us understand which utilities are most critical to our users.

Cookies & Storage: 4SP uses LocalStorage and SessionStorage extensively to maintain application state, handle client-side routing, and store authentication tokens provided by Firebase. We do not sell your data to advertising networks.

4. Third-Party Services Integration

Our platform relies on several trusted third-party providers. By using 4SP, you also acknowledge the privacy practices of these entities:

  • Google Firebase: Hosts our database (Firestore), authentication (Auth), and file storage (Cloud Storage).
  • Cloudflare / Vercel: Manages our DNS, CDN, and edge routing to ensure the network remains unblockable.
  • Stripe: (If applicable) Processes secure donations or premium tier subscriptions. 4SP does not store raw credit card details; all financial data is handled entirely by Stripe.

5. The Right to Disappear (Account Deletion)

You possess complete autonomy over your digital footprint on the network.

Through the Settings page, you can execute a full account deletion. Initiating this process triggers a Cloud Function that irreversibly purges your Firestore profile, releases your reserved username, deletes your posts/messages, and destroys your Firebase Authentication record. Local on-device data is also cleared simultaneously.
